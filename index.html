Mini Biography: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an aerospace scientist, teacher, author, and the 11th President of India.

Early Life

He grew up in Rameswaram and developed a deep curiosity for science, reading, and hard work from an early age.

Important Achievement

He played a major role in India's space and missile programmes and helped mentor young minds across the country.

Many people remember him as the People's President for his approachable nature and focus on youth.

One trait that stood out was lifelong learning.
